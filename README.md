My submission to The Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2, Read Between the Wires is an extremely unique and logic-filled wire-cutting puzzle game with a twist—you can't even trust the rules.

Race to defuse 10 bombs in as little time as possible—see if you can do better than your friends!

## Controls

Click or use the number keys to cut the wires, press tab to focus or unfocus the notes, and try not to die.
You can type in the notes or drag around the numbers to the right to organize your thoughts.
You can also click on one of the rules to mark if you think it's wrong.

## Features

* A unique style of puzzle where you can't even trust the clues you're given
* Thought provoking challenges with the perfect amount of difficulty
* Retro-inspired pixel art graphics

## Behind the Scenes

Read Between the Wires was created with Godot version 4.7.2, and the development journey was an interesting one. This was my first game completely driven by UI, so I was straying a bit far from my usual style of platformers. This was also the first time that I decided I was going to attempt making all of the assets from scratch, and I'm really surprised I was able to pull it off. I taught myself so much about art, music, and sound design, and this is why I love game jams—they're great opportunities to try something new.
